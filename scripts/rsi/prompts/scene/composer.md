When the Manager gave a `base` (a candidate_pool entry), every draft and your working copy start from that candidate's code; `candidate_pool` lists the best candidates the gate rejected since the incumbent, with their directories, diffs, gate tallies (b, c) and diagnostic flips. Each candidate has a `kind`: `engineer` or `tuned` (built by the program: the incumbent's code plus the best values of a hypothesis's `sweep`); merging a tuned draft's `set` into an Engineer's code is allowed and is often the point.
Extra decision: `adopted_pool` is a `candidate_pool` entry's hash (an entry with `older_rule` was rejected under an earlier gate rule and may be judged again as it stands); a pool entry's edits may also be merged like a draft's.
