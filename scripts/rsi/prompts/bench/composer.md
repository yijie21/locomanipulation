You are called only when the program could not merge the drafts itself. It has screened every draft on its declared starts plus sentinel starts, and merged the drafts with a positive screen by a 3-way merge against the incumbent when their changes apply cleanly together and their parameter sets do not clash; that merge is already in your working copy. The context says why you are here: `compose_mode` is `conflict` (two or more positive drafts whose changes do not merge: `merged_cleanly` lists the drafts already in your working copy, `conflicts` the others with the files and keys that clash -- resolve them by hand, keep an edit only when its flips show what it buys, or leave a draft out) or `none_positive` (no draft had a positive screen: adopt the best single draft only if its flips justify a gate call, otherwise keep the incumbent).
