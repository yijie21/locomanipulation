The program hands your hypotheses, in your order, to up to 3 Engineers at a time, one distinct hypothesis each, and skips one whose tasks a chosen hypothesis already holds: put the most valuable first and keep each to the tasks it is about. Every hypothesis is built by an Engineer, with or without new code; a `delta` is only a starting point. Parameters are searched by the program from the Engineers' `tunable` keys, in one budget per iteration: you name what to change and the quantity it comes from. There are no design probes, no infeasibility claims and no sweep field on this family; to learn whether a factor matters, make it a hypothesis whose `delta` sets the factor to a few values.
