`imagined` in {CONTEXT} is a further, weaker kind of evidence. Each candidate was replayed on a fast kinematic model with no physics: every walk and reach lands with an error drawn from real arrival errors of similar commands, several times per start. It sees whether the robot and its hand can get to where the code sends them. It does not see force: a slip, a knocked object or a grip that fails under load is invisible to it, so it can say "this does not get there" and can never say "this grasps". Its failure text comes from the same geometric check.

Its use is the starts no real round runs on: `extra_starts` further starts, none of them a diagnostic or gate start. Per candidate: `doubtful`, the further starts where few draws succeed, with why (`why`: how many draws failed and how; `body_p_miss`: the odds, from a table of real reaches, that the hand misses from the body pose the code stands in, which also lowers a start whose draws all succeed); `per_task`, the same as counts per task; `check_on_diagnostic_starts`, on the diagnostic starts, how many it would have called doubtful and how many of those were real wins, beside the same two numbers for the starts it did not doubt.

What it has been measured to be worth. On starts it had never seen, 640 real rounds of 16 earlier composed candidates: a doubtful start was won 58% of the time and the others 78%. On the diagnostic starts of earlier drafts: drafts that win under half their starts, 13% against 49%; drafts that win 70% or more, 50% against 80%. Of the real failures on those unseen starts it called doubtful: `pinch_reach` 8 of 15, `miss_near` 3 of 4, `knocked` 8 of 11, `too_wide` 15 of 54, `slip` 12 of 37, `side_reach` 5 of 25, falls 1 of 17. A candidate's count of doubtful starts did not predict its gate result (rank correlation 0.17 over 16 candidates), so do not rank candidates by it.

Real rounds outweigh it wherever both exist. One doubtful start is weak. A block of doubtful starts sharing a task and a cause is a warning that a candidate's fixes on the diagnostic starts may not carry over, and worth finding in its code. It cannot earn an edit its place: an edit still needs a trace moment on a start it fixed. It is not a reason to answer `keep` on its own.
