# grasp_atomic

**Cue:** A pick (or place) target sits on a shelf or bay whose own slab top is 2-20 cm above the robot's idle hanging-hand height (about 0.69-0.71 m), and the grasp point is above that height. The closest-stance walk ends with a hanging hand 3-6 cm inside or under that slab. Signs to look for: the same plan succeeds on some spawns and fails on others, depending on how deep the hand landed; failures read 'nothing between the fingers' or a pre-reach that stalls 20+ cm off while the wrist presses the slab's front-underside edge.

**Lesson:** Fix it at stance choice, not after the walk. In the stance chooser (pick._choose over stances()), predict both hanging jaws at the walk's end: pelvis + 0.25 m*a +/- 0.13 m*left(a) at z 0.69 m, and the same points 0.05 m further along a. Skip any stance that puts either point inside the footprint of the object's own support when that support's top is 0.02-0.20 m above 0.69 m. Apply this only on shelves and only for grasp points above 0.69 m, and fall back to the unfiltered choice when no stance survives. Do not count other slabs; in this run that re-planned lower-shelf plants and lost one, though that rests on a single fresh pair. Do not get the effect by raising D_MIN globally (0.36 -> 0.44 broke 4 starts) or with a post-walk back-out retract (fixed 0). Check before gating: an offline choose() replay over fresh starts should re-plan only the targeted shelf object (here 25/25 wolf starts, 0/25 on every other task), and no diagnostic success should change its plan.

**Evidence:** Rows iter 4 'hand_hooked stance filter' (helped), iter 2 'D_MIN_M 0.44' (hurt), iter 2 'entry() retract' (no observed effect). The stance-time filter fixed DisplayWolf s410029 in all three runs that carried it (iterations 3, 4, 5) with no diagnostic break. Fresh closed-loop pairs went 3 fail -> OK and 1 OK both ways. On the sealed pool DisplayWolf rose 2/5 -> 5/5 between the iteration 1 and iteration 5 incumbents, and every other non-mug task was identical. The other pieces in its bundle cannot fire on the wolf (front clearance needs D >= 0.54, the wolf plans D 0.46; the tight tier runs only when no 0.003 stance exists). By contrast, raising D_MIN to 0.44 fixed the same wolf start but broke Plant x2, TvRemote and Vase (15 -> 12). The post-walk retract (iteration 2) fixed 0 of 30 and went b 0, c 1 at the gate.

**Confidence:** medium. The mechanism is backed by contact-logged reruns, a same-plan 3 cm vs 6 cm split, and an independent sealed gain. The attribution inside the bundle rests on code-path isolation and an offline replay, not on a gate run of the filter alone. All of it comes from one object on one shelf geometry (the 0.84 m wolf shelf, ~3 diagnostic, 4 fresh and 5 sealed starts). The hanging-jaw numbers belong to the shared motor layer, so they should carry over, but the 0.02-0.20 m slab band has been tested on only that one slab.
