# pick_single

**Cue:** An object much flatter than the gripper's drop below the jaw centre (about 3 cm tall or less) lies on a low support (about 0.45 m). Its only side that fits the jaw forces either a side entry along the object's long axis or a wrist yaw more than 40° from the body facing. Side grasps are refused or stall 10–15 cm short of the grasp, and a longer entry timeout changes no outcome.

**Lesson:** In pick.py, send such objects to a wrist-down pinch instead of the side-grasp program (is_top → pick_top). Gate: box half-height < 0.02 m AND support top < 0.6 m. Wrist R = rot_z(ψ)·rot_y(π/2), where ψ closes the jaw across the narrow side, chosen nearest the body facing, with no facing-tolerance limit. Jaw centre at the object's xy, at support top + 0.033 m (fingertip length) + 0.004 m. Pregrasp 0.10 m straight above (through the over-the-edge via point); descend at 0.05 m/s, grip, lift 0.10 m; count success only if the object is between the fingers. Add a fallback pass with its own depth limit (0.39 m vs 0.35 m for side grasps). Keep the gate narrow. The closing side must leave about 1 cm or more per side in the open jaw: the remote, 3.6 cm in the 9.48 cm jaw, worked; the wallet, 8.7 cm with about 4 mm per side, went 0/6. Do not extend it to taller objects or supports of 0.75 m and up: mugs on 0.75–0.92 m supports came out 41–58° off, 0/7, and lost 2 side-grasp successes. Check: a dry plan diff must change only is_top objects' plans.

**Evidence:** Iteration 2, candidate acd864174766: is_top matched only the TV remote, and diag remotes went 0/6→5/6 with 0 broken across the 50 starts. Sealed remote went 0/10 (e8ce52c71938)→10/10 (acd864174766); the candidate's other parts (MAX_DEPTH 0.35, deeper close) flipped only canister, dish soap and jam starts in their own probes. Gate b=8, c=0. Controls showing the side entry is blocked by geometry, not slow: entry timeout 9→20 s gave 11→11 (the remotes stayed 14.4 / 11.4 cm short) and 25→25 in iteration 4. Iteration 0's FACE_TOLERANCE 80 probe added no win and one fall, but 3 of the top-down remote wins were planned 68–87° from the facing. Iteration 3: the 0.39 m top-down depth added remote s100033 (19→20). Bounding failures in iteration 4: widening the gate to the mugs (TOP_FLAT 0.05, TOP_SURFACE_MAX 0.8) gave 25→23, 0/7, 2 broken; the wallet at 1.12 m (TOP_SURFACE_MAX 1.2) gave 0/6.

**Confidence:** High for the operation itself on flat objects with a wide jaw margin on a ~0.45 m support: one mechanism, isolated by construction, 5/6 diag and 10/10 sealed, 0 broken. Medium for the bounds: success was seen at only one support height (0.45 m), and the failures at 0.75–1.12 m mix support height with object height (mug) and jaw margin (wallet), so the 0.6 m and 1 cm-per-side limits are inferred, not measured one at a time.
