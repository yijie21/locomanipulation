# r1_slide_then_grasp

**Cue:** A thin object stands packed in a row under an overhead shelf or surface, so only one box side (its thickness, under about 6 cm and well under the open jaw) fits the two-finger jaw, and a top-down pinch has no headroom. The level side grasp closes on the object's front few cm, far ahead of its centre of mass. The failures read 'knocked in entry', 'not lifted (rise 2-4 cm)' or 'lost pad contact during the lift'. Near-lifts of 2-4 cm, where the object pivots about its rear-bottom edge, are the tell.

**Lesson:** Do not change the global constants. Give the object its own branch in pick.pick_once, using a predicate like narrow_covered: cover.overhead(X), and exactly one box side <= JAW_SPAN_M that is narrower than 0.06 m. Pass these values to grasp_program.run as call arguments:
- open jaw just wider than the side: JAW_OPEN 0.004 (default 0.0245)
- entry stops 5 cm short of the centre: entry_capture_depth_m 0.05 and entry_overshoot_m -0.05, so the jaw ends 3-4.6 cm from the centre instead of 7-8 cm. 7.5 cm gave 0-1/25; 6 cm fixed one stall but broke one success.
- lateral_correction_max_m 0, entry-gate hold 0, disturb_limit_m 0.10
- about twice the squeeze: JAW_CLOSED -0.06, about 20 N instead of 10 N
- rise_min 0.015 (not 0.05), so a pivoted object still goes on to the pull
Carry a pull on the outcome (info 'withdraw'). In held.pick_held, pull the jaw straight back, level, 0.30 m before any walk. Continue to at most 0.35 m only while cover.overhead(X) still holds. Do not re-pull on a 'centre < 3 cm past the edge' bar: that re-pulled objects already clear and dropped one.
Checks:
- every other pick's steps are byte-identical to the incumbent's record (as globals, these values made an 8.7 cm object ungraspable)
- log per round: jaw-to-centre offset at the close, rise, pull distance and past_edge_cm

**Evidence:** Accepted candidate fba81e5762ec (iteration 1): book diag 0/25 -> 22/25 with 0 broken; gate b=29, c=0, p=0.0 (stage 1 29/30 vs 0/30, no falls or drops); sealed books 0/40 -> 38/40, wallet unchanged (0/40). The same values as globals (probe H1) gave 0 -> 21/25. The contrast that shows the deep, squeezed hold matters: iteration 0's shallow captures (7.5 cm, normal squeeze, RISE_MIN 0.05) gave 0/25 and 1/25 with narrow jaw, with about 9 rounds stuck at 2-4 cm rises. Not isolated: which of the four simultaneous changes (depth 7.5 -> 5 cm, squeeze -0.02 -> -0.06, RISE_MIN 0.05 -> 0.015, pull 0.20 -> 0.30 m) carries the gain. Lateral correction off vs on is 21 vs 19 (4 starts differ). The pull_on to 0.35 m rests on one start (s150030 fixed, s150042 dropped under the alternative trigger). Capture at 6 cm: +1 / -1.

**Confidence:** medium. The whole bundle is strongly supported on three independent pools (diag 22/0, gate 29/0, sealed 38/40) with zero breaks. But it is one object type on one shelf row, and no run separates the depth, squeeze, rise-threshold and pull changes, so apply them together and do not credit any single one.
