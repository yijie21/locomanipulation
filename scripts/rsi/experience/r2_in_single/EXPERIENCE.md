# r2_in_single

**Cue:** A side grasp is pitched nose-down by one fixed constant only so the wrist and forearm clear the support top on entry. The same pitch is then held through the carry into a drop or placement whose target is at or past the arm's standing reach (about 0.7 m or more ahead of the pelvis). Objects leave the jaw during that over-reach, and the over's attitude error grows with the grasp pitch.

**Lesson:** Make the grasp pitch depend on what is under the hand, per approach, instead of lowering the constant. In pick.grasp_pitch, pass the approach yaw and check the hand's strip for fixed objects on the support (strip_blocker: STRIP_HALF_M either side of the approach line, STRIP_BACK_M back). If the strip is clear, use the least whole degree from 1 to GRASP_PITCH_DEG at which min_jaw(pitch, gripper + wrist low points) is at most the object centre's height above the support (here 4 deg instead of 15 for centres 4.0-4.1 cm up). If a fixed object is in the strip (here a 2.5-2.7 cm dish), keep the full 15 deg so those rounds are unchanged. Do not set 4 or 8 deg globally. Check: log the pitch chosen per start; every strip-blocked start must be step-identical to the incumbent. Watch: at a 0.80 m reach a level hold ended 4.6-7.6 cm short of the opening centre. A start that only succeeded because the pitched object slipped in can then break unless the release rule admits that shortfall.

**Evidence:** Iteration 1 probe, global pitch 15 -> 4 deg: diag 10 -> 11. It fixed the 3 predicted deep apples (010/020/035) and broke the 2 predicted dish-side oranges (003/018), where the wrist hit the fixed dish's wall. Iteration 1 candidate, strip-aware least pitch as its only mechanism: diag 13/49 vs 10, the same 3 fixed, 0 broken. Held-out gate: 19/60 vs 16, b 3 / c 0, p 0.125 (rejected for power). Contrast, iteration 0's global 8 deg bundled with two other changes: gate b 3 / c 2 plus a new fall, and the Engineer traced the dish-side stall and knock to the 8-deg housing dropping below the dish rim. Supporting but correlational, pooled over the incumbent and 7 probes: apples held level had a median 2.5 deg over-attitude error and 39/45 succeeded; apples at 15 deg had 25.6 deg and 10/24. Counter-evidence: iteration 2's layer test stacked least pitch on via -0.25 / release -0.035; it fixed 020 and broke 010, net 0, because the level apple ended 7.2 cm short.

**Confidence:** low. The 'condition on what is under the strip, don't go global' part is well supported: deterministic per-start contrasts on two pools, the predicted breaks went from 2 to 0, and blocked starts stayed bit-identical. The gain is not: only 3 held-out starts, the gate never accepted it, and it traded a start when stacked on a different drop path.
