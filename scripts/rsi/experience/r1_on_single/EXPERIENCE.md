# r1_on_single

**Cue:** A put-down skill places an upright object gripped from the side by a parallel jaw on a position-servoed arm. Some put-downs reach the target and then end tilted or toppled ('not resting on target', or counted as drops when the target sits well below the source). Moving the spot inward, pressing down, or changing the retract height leaves that count unchanged.

**Lesson:** Make the release in the put-down (place.py _lower_onto / _release) closed-loop, for side grasps only, and leave top-down pinches on the old path. (1) On the 'over' and 'lower' legs, command the wrist turned by the least rotation that takes the object's measured up axis (R(q_now) R(q_rest)^T z) to vertical, clamped to 25 deg. Before this, objects arrived tilted 6-16 deg; after it, 0-4 deg. (2) Once lowered (underside about 1.5 cm above the top), pop the arm servo's position lead and turn integral and re-command the hand at its measured jaw pose before opening. Otherwise the opening jaw springs up 1.5-4.3 cm and leaves the object leaning on one finger. (3) After opening, if the object centre is off by more than 2 mm along the closing axis (wrist y), move the open jaw by that offset, at most 3 cm. (4) Back out 12 cm along -wrist x at the held attitude with no rise, and only then rise 5 cm. To check: log the lean at the end of 'lower' and after the retract. The 'not resting on target' count should go to zero with no new breaks.

**Evidence:** Iteration 4 (8937ab5c8d97) -> iteration 5 (9da5a5e404f4) differ only in this release: pick.py, grasp_program.py and __init__.py are byte-identical. Accept pool 16 -> 19/60, drops 3 -> 0, against the same incumbent b=8/c=1 -> b=10/c=0. The only failure strings that disappeared are the 3 'not resting on target' put-downs (jam x2, vase), and nothing else changed. Diag: 0 broken, jam s110013 restored. Negative controls: inward spot containment (PLACE_SIDE_MIN_M 0.06, iteration 4) left 3 topples, the same as c96a (iteration 3). RETRACT_UP_M 0 gave the same flips as without it (iteration 5 vs iteration 3 probes). PLACE_CLEAR_M -0.01 and RETRACT_UP_M -0.02 did not fix the reproduced topples. Sealed 13 -> 22/80 (vase 0->5, soap 3->6), but that also includes iteration 4's other changes.

**Confidence:** medium. The contrast is a single isolated mechanism, it removed every topple, broke nothing, and the topple class was predicted beforehand. But it rests on 3 accept starts and 1 diag start in a deterministic sim. Which sub-step matters is known from one start only: on jam s110013, servo-lead clearing alone or centring alone each sufficed and levelling alone did not. Keep all four steps until another family ablates them.
