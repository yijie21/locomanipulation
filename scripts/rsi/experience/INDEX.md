# Experience library

One entry per finished family (scripts/rsi/reflect.py, TODO 32.10): what each change did, and at most one lesson that may transfer. An entry's numbers come from its own family's objects and surfaces: treat them as hypotheses here and check them on this family's rounds.

| family | cue | confidence |
|---|---|---|
| [grasp_atomic](grasp_atomic/EXPERIENCE.md) | A pick (or place) target sits on a shelf or bay whose own slab top is 2-20 cm above the robot's idle hanging-hand height (about 0.69-0.71 m), and the grasp point is above that height. The closest-stance walk ends with a hanging hand 3-6 cm inside or under that slab. Signs to look for: the same plan succeeds on some spawns and fails on others, depending on how deep the hand landed; failures read 'nothing between the fingers' or a pre-reach that stalls 20+ cm off while the wrist presses the slab's front-underside edge. | medium |
| [pick_single](pick_single/EXPERIENCE.md) | An object much flatter than the gripper's drop below the jaw centre (about 3 cm tall or less) lies on a low support (about 0.45 m). Its only side that fits the jaw forces either a side entry along the object's long axis or a wrist yaw more than 40° from the body facing. Side grasps are refused or stall 10–15 cm short of the grasp, and a longer entry timeout changes no outcome. | High for the operation itself on flat objects with a wide jaw margin on a ~0 |
| [r1_held_near_single](r1_held_near_single/EXPERIENCE.md) | A carry or approach goal needs the robot (holding an object) to stand within about 1 m of a target, but the target is boxed in by furniture. The gaps between pieces are narrower than 2× the planner's body radius, so the end-stance search on the standard inflated nav map returns 0 free or 0 routed cells within reach. The pieces lining the gap are low (their tops are well below the hands and the held object), so in reality only the legs could touch them. | medium |
| [r1_held_single](r1_held_single/EXPERIENCE.md) | A side grasp of a short object on an open support with nothing overhanging it. The object's centre sits closer to the support top than the gripper's housing/palm hangs below the jaw axis. Symptoms: the hand stalls at the support edge or never leaves the pre-grasp ('entry timed out N cm from the grasp'), or knocks the object. Controller-side probes (slower entry, entry gate off, wider tracking clip) flip nothing. | medium |
| [r1_in_single](r1_in_single/EXPERIENCE.md) | A side grasp of a low object: centre less than about 4.5 cm above its support's top, lying deep enough that the forearm crosses the support's edge (about 0.17 m or more in on a straight approach). The entry times out short or the pre-grasp misses, and the hand ends up pitched nose-up with the jaw risen. Contacts posed from the trace put a wrist or forearm link on the slab's edge, not the fingers or the gripper housing. | medium |
| [r1_on_single](r1_on_single/EXPERIENCE.md) | A put-down skill places an upright object gripped from the side by a parallel jaw on a position-servoed arm. Some put-downs reach the target and then end tilted or toppled ('not resting on target', or counted as drops when the target sits well below the source). Moving the spot inward, pressing down, or changing the retract height leaves that count unchanged. | medium |
| [r1_slide_then_grasp](r1_slide_then_grasp/EXPERIENCE.md) | A thin object stands packed in a row under an overhead shelf or surface, so only one box side (its thickness, under about 6 cm and well under the open jaw) fits the two-finger jaw, and a top-down pinch has no headroom. The level side grasp closes on the object's front few cm, far ahead of its centre of mass. The failures read 'knocked in entry', 'not lifted (rise 2-4 cm)' or 'lost pad contact during the lift'. Near-lifts of 2-4 cm, where the object pivots about its rear-bottom edge, are the tell. | medium |
| [r2_in_single](r2_in_single/EXPERIENCE.md) | A side grasp is pitched nose-down by one fixed constant only so the wrist and forearm clear the support top on entry. The same pitch is then held through the carry into a drop or placement whose target is at or past the arm's standing reach (about 0.7 m or more ahead of the pelvis). Objects leave the jaw during that over-reach, and the over's attitude error grows with the grasp pitch. | low |
| [r2_on_single](r2_on_single/EXPERIENCE.md) | A side-grasp pick that aims the jaw centre at the object centre fails only on the longer objects, while shorter objects with the same skill succeed. The failures read 'knocked/disturbed N cm in entry', or the traces show the object moving while the jaw is still about the capture depth plus 0.5 cm short. Cause: the object's half-length along the approach axis (the box axis the jaw does not span) exceeds the gripper housing's offset behind the jaw centre plus the entry's capture depth. | Two levels |
