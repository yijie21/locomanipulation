# r2_on_single

**Cue:** A side-grasp pick that aims the jaw centre at the object centre fails only on the longer objects, while shorter objects with the same skill succeed. The failures read 'knocked/disturbed N cm in entry', or the traces show the object moving while the jaw is still about the capture depth plus 0.5 cm short. Cause: the object's half-length along the approach axis (the box axis the jaw does not span) exceeds the gripper housing's offset behind the jaw centre plus the entry's capture depth.

**Lesson:** In the side-grasp entry (place.place_on's pick: place.PICK_CAPTURE_DEPTH_M; the held(X) pick uses 0.025), take for each failing object a = its box half-extent along the approach, and h = the housing-front offset behind the jaw centre (Dex1: 0.0407 m). If a > h + capture, raise the entry capture depth to at least a - h + 0.005 m, per object or as a constant that covers the family. Keep it at or under the carry's held-out limit (place.HELD_OUT_MAX_M 0.03). 0.015 -> 0.025 cleared a = 6.15 cm (canister) and, together with a creep budget matched to the object's mass, a = 6.55 cm (wolf). If a - h is above the held-out limit (a > about 7.1 cm with this gripper: books 8.3-8.5 cm, plate holder 13.9 cm), no centred side grasp exists. Do not loosen HELD_OUT_MAX_M or the capture depth to chase it; park the task for a new action (end grasp with a lever check, push, two hands). Check after the change: in the FK traces the object does not move before the jaw is within the capture depth, and the held-out after the close stays under 3 cm.

**Evidence:** Iteration 0 probe H1 changed only PICK_CAPTURE_DEPTH_M 0.015 -> 0.025: diag 4 -> 8/50, fixed canister s04/s14/s24/s35 (4 of 4 predicted), broke 0. In all 4 incumbent canister failures the object began moving with the jaw 1.8-2.0 cm from its centre. With the creep change (probe H2) the wolf flipped 4/5. The shipped candidate got gate b=11, c=0, p=0.00049, and sealed went canister 0/8 -> 3/8, wolf 0/8 -> 7/8. Upper bound: claims C3/C4 (books, jaw at least 4.24-4.43 cm from the centre; all 10 picks measured 4.6-6.3 cm out) and C2 (plate holder, 9.8 cm). The book parameters with HELD_OUT 0.07 (iteration 0 probe H3) flipped 0 of 15 book starts. The C2 end-grasp attempt sets finished 0. The iteration 1 Reviewer's book prototype lost both books (one slid from 4.6 to 6.1 cm out and fell in the carry). Sealed books and plate-holder starts stayed 0 or unchanged through the whole run.

**Confidence:** Two levels. High for raising the capture depth to a - h + 0.5 cm when a is within h + 3 cm: a single-parameter probe flipped 4 of 4 predicted starts with 0 broken, FK measured the mechanism, and the gate and sealed pools agree. Medium for the upper-bound clause (park when a - h exceeds the held-out limit): it rests on claim attempts and 2 prototype rounds, not on a gated contrast. h = 4.07 cm belongs to this robot's Dex1 gripper; re-derive it for another gripper.
