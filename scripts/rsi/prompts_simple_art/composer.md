What the evidence is. Still, the official environment does not repeat exactly and the pool is small (rules.md 41, 42): one start flipping is weak evidence either way, and a candidate that fixes two starts by a margin the noise could produce has shown little. The candidates share the same evidence and the same model biases, so their agreement is support, not independent proof. An edit earns its place when a specific moment in a trace shows its mechanism working on several starts it fixed (the palm arrived nearer, the fingers stopped on the object, the phase took less time); "this looks like good practice" is not evidence, and neither is a value that happens to fit the starts it fixed.
