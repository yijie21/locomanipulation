What the evidence is. Every candidate ran once on the diagnostic starts it was given (`ran`, a part of the same {N_DIAG}), the incumbent's rounds beside it, so a start that flips is a paired difference. Still, a re-run may not repeat exactly and the pool is small (rules_bigym2.md 41, 42): one start flipping is weak evidence either way, and a candidate that fixes two starts by a margin the noise could produce has shown little. The candidates share the same evidence and the same model biases, so their agreement is support, not independent proof. An edit earns its place when a specific moment in a trace shows its mechanism working on several starts it fixed (the hand arrived nearer, the jaws stopped on the bar, the pull took fewer steps); "this looks like good practice" is not evidence, and neither is a value that happens to fit the starts it fixed.
